Brainstorm a comprehensive list of questions that, when answered, will provide actionable insights for business decision-making. 
These questions should bridge the gap between technical data and business strategy by focusing on "why" and "how" scenarios. 
Ensure each question is directly tied back to the initial problem statement to maintain focus. 
Prioritize these questions based on their potential impact on the business and the feasibility of answering them with available data.
This list serves as a roadmap for the analysis phase.

Key Analytical Questions (Cafe Sales Case Example):
    1. Overall Impact: By what percentage did revenue and order volume drop compared to previous quarters and year-over-year?
    2. Geographic Variance: Which geographic locations or individual stores experienced the steepest decline?
    3. Product Performance: Did specific menu categories (e.g., beverages vs. bakery items) drive the sales drop?
    4. Customer Behavior: Has Average Order Value (AOV) declined, or is the drop driven primarily by lower customer footfall/visit frequency?
    5. Price Sensitivity: Were price revisions or discount promotions correlated with changes in demand elasticity?
